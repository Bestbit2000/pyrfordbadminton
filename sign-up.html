What happens next

  1. Someone from the club gets back to you about your first Thursday.
  2. Come along to Woking Leisure Centre, 8‑10pm, with your racket and indoor sports shoes. We provide the shuttles.
  3. Play two sessions free. If you'd like to stay, membership is £70 a quarter.

We only use your details to arrange your visit and to keep in touch about the club.
