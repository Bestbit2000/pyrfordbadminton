A friendly club for adults who play at a social or intermediate standard. Two hours on court every week, good games, no league table, and a few socials off court through the year.

Why Pyrford?

The club began on 1 July 1982 as an informal group of players in Pyrford. Nine years later to the day, on 1 July 1991, it became a formal club. It later moved to Woking Leisure Centre, and kept the name it started with.
