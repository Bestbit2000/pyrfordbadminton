Pyrford Badminton Club is a social club for adults of all ages. We play every Thursday, 8‑10pm, at Woking Leisure Centre. Your first two sessions are free.

Is it for you?

We're a club for people who enjoy a good game and good company. Nobody comes to win a trophy.

You'll fit right in if

  • You're an adult, of any age
  • You play at a social or intermediate standard
  • You want friendly games rather than playing in competitions
  • You'd like a club with a social life off the court too

Somewhere else might suit you better if

  • You're under 18: try Lightwater Junior Badminton Club
  • You're a beginner and starting out on your badminton journey: try the No Strings Badminton sessions at Woking Leisure Centre, which are open to all abilities
  • You're looking for league or competitive badminton: try Martlet Badminton Club in Horsell, which plays on Friday evenings and has league teams
40+years, since 1982

More than 40 years of Thursday nights

Pyrford Badminton Club started on 1 July 1982 and has been playing fun, friendly badminton on Thursdays ever since. It began in Pyrford as an informal group of players and became a formal club on 1 July 1991. It has since moved to Woking Leisure Centre, but kept the Pyrford name.

We also get together off the court, with regular social events through the year.
