Friendly badminton in Woking, every Thursday night

Pyrford Badminton Club is a social club for adults of all ages. We play every Thursday, 8‑10pm, at Woking Leisure Centre. Your first two sessions are free.

Is it for you?

We're a club for people who enjoy a good game and good company. Nobody comes to win a trophy.

You'll fit right in if

  • You're an adult, of any age
  • You play at a social or intermediate standard
  • You want friendly games rather than competitive matches
  • You'd like a club with a social life off the court too

Somewhere else might suit you better if

  • You're under 18: try Lightwater Junior Badminton Club
  • You're looking for league or competitive badminton

Forty years of Thursday nights

Pyrford Badminton Club started in 1986 and has been playing fun, friendly badminton every Thursday ever since.

We also get together off the court, with regular social events through the year.
