Thursdays 8–10pm

Friendly, social badminton for adults in Woking. Playing since 1982.

Who plays

Adults of any age over 18, at a social or intermediate standard, who would rather have a friendly game than a competitive one. Off the court, there are social events through the year.

Woking Leisure Centre

Kingfield Road, Woking GU22 9BA, run by Freedom Leisure. Free parking for two hours, with plenty of spaces. Map

Bring

  • Your own racket The club has no spares
  • Indoor sports shoes
  • A drink of water
  • Shuttles Provided by the club

44 years of club nights

It began on 1 July 1982 as an informal group of players in Pyrford, became a formal club on 1 July 1991, and later moved to Woking Leisure Centre, keeping the Pyrford name.
