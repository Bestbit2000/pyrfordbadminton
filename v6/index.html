Thursday night badminton

A friendly club for adults in Woking who play at a social or intermediate standard. Proper games, good company, no league table.

Over the net
in three steps

  1. 1

    Fill in the sign-up form

    Say a little about how you play. Someone from the club gets back to you about your first Thursday.

    Open the sign-up form
  2. 2

    Play two sessions free

    Bring your own racket (the club has no spares), indoor sports shoes and a drink of water. The shuttles are provided.

  3. 3

    Stay, and pay the membership

    The price is set to pay for the courts, so it depends on how many members there are.
