Come and play

Joining takes three steps, and you get two Thursdays on court before you pay anything.

How to join

  1. Sign up

    Fill in the short sign-up form and tell us a little about how you play. We'll get back to you with everything you need for your first night.

  2. Play two free sessions

    Come along on two Thursdays, 8‑10pm, at Woking Leisure Centre. Meet the members and see if the club suits you.

  3. Join for £70 a quarter

    If you'd like to stay, membership is £70 a quarter.

2 free sessions

What it costs

Your first two sessions are free. After that, membership is £70 a quarter. Over a 13-week quarter that's roughly £5.40 a night.

What to bring

  • Your own racket (we don't have spares)
  • Indoor sports shoes
  • A drink of water

We provide the shuttles. Parking at the leisure centre is free for 2 hours, and there are plenty of spaces.

Anything else you'd like to know? Ask us on the sign-up form.

Who the club is for

We're adults of all ages who play at a social or intermediate standard and enjoy friendly games rather than competition.

Under 18? Lightwater Junior Badminton Club runs sessions for younger players.

Just starting out? The No Strings Badminton sessions at Woking Leisure Centre are open to all abilities.

Looking for league badminton? Martlet Badminton Club in Horsell has league teams and plays on Friday evenings.
