What happens next
  1. Someone from the club gets back to you about your first Thursday.
  2. Come to Woking Leisure Centre, 8‑10pm, with your racket and indoor sports shoes. We provide the shuttles.
  3. Try it out for free for 2 weeks. It's a trial on both sides: you see if the club suits you, and we see if you suit the club.
  4. If it's a good fit both ways, we'll offer you a place. Membership is £70 a quarter.

We only use your details to arrange your visit and to keep in touch about the club.
