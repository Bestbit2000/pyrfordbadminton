Questions

Can I try it before I join?

Yes, you can try it out for free for 2 weeks. The trial works both ways: you see if the club suits you, and we see if you suit the club. If it's a good fit, we'll offer you a place. Fill in the sign-up form to start.

What standard do I need to be?

Social or intermediate. Our Thursday nights are about friendly games, not competition.

I'm a beginner. Is the club right for me?

Our Thursday nights suit players who already play at a social or intermediate standard. If you're just starting out, try the No Strings Badminton sessions at Woking Leisure Centre, which are open to all abilities.

I want league or competitive badminton. Where should I go?

Our Thursday nights are social rather than competitive. Martlet Badminton Club in Horsell has league teams and plays on Friday evenings.

Can under-18s join?

The club is for adults. Younger players can try Lightwater Junior Badminton Club.

Why is it called Pyrford if you play in Woking?

The club started in Pyrford on 1 July 1982 and later moved to Woking Leisure Centre, keeping its name. That's more than 40 years of Thursday nights.

How do I get in touch?

Use the sign-up form. You can ask a question there too.
