Who it’s for

Which side of the net are you on?

You’ll fit in here if

  • you’re 18 or over. There’s no upper age limit.
  • you play at a social or intermediate standard.
  • you’d rather have a good, friendly game than a competitive one.
  • you’d enjoy a social off the court now and then. We hold them through the year.

You may be happier elsewhere if

  • you’re under 18. Try Lightwater Junior Badminton Club.
  • you’re new to the game. Try No Strings Badminton, open to all abilities, in the same leisure centre.
  • you want league matches. Try Martlet Badminton Club in Horsell, which plays on Friday evenings and has league teams.

Kit

Bring three things. We bring the shuttles.

  1. Your own racket. The club has no spares.
  2. Indoor sports shoes.
  3. A drink of water.

History

The first of July, twice.

1 July 1982
A few players start meeting in Pyrford, informally.
1 July 1991
Nine years to the day later, they become a formal club.
Since then
We moved to Woking Leisure Centre and kept the Pyrford name.

Book a free night

We use your details only to arrange your visit and to keep in touch about the club.

  1. Fill in the sign-up form with a little about how you play. Someone from the club gets back to you about your first Thursday.

  2. Play two sessions free and see whether we suit you.

  3. Pay the membership if you’d like to stay.
